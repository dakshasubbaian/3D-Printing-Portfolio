# 3D-Printed Quadruped Robot

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the fabrication and assembly of a quadruped robot using 3D printing technology. The robot features four articulated legs with servo motors to enable controlled joint movement and support walking mechanisms.

Developed as part of a college project, the prototype combines additive manufacturing, mechanical design and robotics.

## Design Motivation

The project was inspired by the locomotion of four-legged organisms. The objective was to develop a lightweight, modular robotic structure capable of supporting articulated movement while exploring the practical applications of 3D printing in robotics.

## Working Principle

The Arduino Nano is designed to control the angular movement of 12 servo motors. Coordinated joint movements enable the robot to perform walking sequences.

- Servo motors
- Mounting screws

## Design and Fabrication

The robot consists of a central chassis connected to four articulated legs. Individual mechanical components were fabricated using 3D printing and assembled with servo motors to form movable joints.

The skeletal structure reduces material consumption while providing space for electronic components and wiring.

## 3D Printing Process

1. Preparation of individual 3D models.
2. Slicing and preparation for printing.
3. Fabrication of mechanical components.
4. Removal of support structures.
5. Post-processing and inspection.
6. Servo motor installation.
7. Final mechanical assembly.

## Working Principle

The robot is designed to achieve locomotion through coordinated angular movement of its servo motors. Each articulated leg can change its position through controlled joint rotation, enabling the development of walking and turning mechanisms.
