# CAD & Additive Manufacturing Portfolio

## About

This repository showcases my work in Computer-Aided Design (CAD), additive manufacturing, mechanical design and rapid prototyping. It includes a published Indian patent application, robotic prototypes, product designs, bio-inspired structures and academic laboratory experiments developed using Autodesk Fusion 360.

## Projects

### 01. [IoT Cableway Transport](./01-Iot-Cableway-Transport)

A smart agricultural transportation system designed to transport harvested produce across hilly and uneven terrains. The system incorporates an inclined cableway, support towers, trolley wheels and a cargo pod, with proposed solar-powered operation and IoT-based monitoring.

The project aims to reduce manual transportation efforts, improve agricultural logistics and minimize post-harvest losses. Motion animation was developed using Fusion 360 to demonstrate the working mechanism.

**Patent Details**
- Indian Patent Application Number: 202641120616
- Status: Published – Awaiting Examination

### 02. [Spider Robot](./02-Robot)

A four-legged robotic prototype developed using 3D-printed mechanical components, Arduino Nano and 12 SG90 servo motors. Each leg consists of three articulated joints designed for coordinated movement.

The project combines additive manufacturing, mechanical assembly, electronics and robotic locomotion.

### 03. [Honeycomb Spider](./03-Honeycomb-Spider)

A bio-inspired hybrid structural design combining honeycomb geometry with spider-web reinforcement patterns.

The design aims to improve strength-to-weight ratio, load distribution, structural stiffness and impact absorption while minimizing material consumption.

### 04. [Power Bank](./04-Power-Bank)

A compact battery-powered portable charging device designed to accommodate internal batteries and electronic components.

The project focuses on space optimization, portability, functional enclosure design and suitability for additive manufacturing.

### 05. [Battery Dispenser](./05-Battery-Dispenser)

A mechanical battery storage and dispensing system designed for organized storage and convenient battery retrieval.

The design focuses on accessibility, efficient material utilization, compactness and ease of manufacturing.

### 06. [Bike Navigation](./06-Bike-Navigation)

A motorcycle-mounted navigation device designed to provide riders with convenient access to navigation information.

The project focuses on mounting stability, compact enclosure design, ergonomics and rider accessibility.

### 07. [College CAD Work](./07-College-CAD-Work)

A collection of mechanical component designs developed as part of academic laboratory experiments.

The models include:

- Piston and connecting rod
- Bearing housing
- Nut and bolt assembly

These experiments demonstrate fundamental CAD modelling techniques, mechanical assemblies and engineering design principles.
